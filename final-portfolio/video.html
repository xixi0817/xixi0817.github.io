Group Work: ANIMATION









This project is a cleaning product commercial advertisement.The Story is about: Two children, a boy and a girl, get a phone call from their parents that they will be coming home sooner than expected. The boy and girl trashed the house while their parents were gone and did not expect them back home so soon. Now they have to clean up their mess, and quick!
