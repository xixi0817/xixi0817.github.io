This project I focused on "Humidity VS desire". Which one is the winner? human beings struggle in their desires. Humans can lose their minds. What are they willing to sacrifice for their desires? Money? Family? Love?

The biblical figure of David was the king of spades, representing the triumph of the righteous over the strong. I designed my King of Spades inside an animal mouth to represent the strength that David is capable of. A wild animal can be very dangerous to everyone, the power of a King(who has absolute power to kill anyone) is also dangerous. The King inside the animal mouth also represents that human beings could be too obsessed with the power. If they can not control the power correctly, they will get in trouble. That is the darkness in humility.
