Portfolio Website

HTML · CSS · JavaScript · Bootstrap  |  Web Design II, City College of New York

A portfolio site for my illustration work, built for Web Design II. I hand-coded every page in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, using Bootstrap for the side menu. The site has an illustrated home page and one page for each project. The projects below were first shown on this site.

The Little Prince

Procreate · Adobe Illustrator

Six digital illustrations of the characters the little prince meets on the asteroids. Each one was sketched in Procreate and finished in Adobe Illustrator. The style was influenced by the children's book illustrator Júlia Sardà.

Drawing process

Procreate records the drawing as it happens. These clips are sped up.

Zhou Shen Album Art

Adobe Illustrator · Photoshop  |  About 4 weeks

Album art for a song by the singer Zhou Shen, in which he voices five characters in a fairy tale: a little girl, the narrator, a young warrior, a dragon, and a king. In the story, a dragon kidnaps the princess and a young warrior sets out to rescue her. The set includes a front and back cover, a poster, and character stickers.

The E-Scooters Revolution

Adobe Illustrator · Photoshop  |  Editorial illustration, about 3 weeks

An editorial illustration and two-page magazine layout for a New Yorker article about e-scooter fleets arriving in New York. The idea came from seeing more delivery workers, teenagers, and couples riding e-scooters on the street during the pandemic.

Space I Am

Cinema 4D  |  3D modeling

A 3D model of my ideal work studio, based on my illustration "Space I Am". The project covered modeling, lighting, shading, texturing, camera composition, and rendering.

Vector Illustration

Adobe Illustrator  |  Vector Illustration class, Spring 2021

Weekly projects from a vector illustration class covering logo design, icons, character design, lettering, 3D illustration, and pattern design. The self-portrait was a line art assignment, where form and depth come from strokes of different weights and angles.
